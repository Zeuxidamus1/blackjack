# Challenger Rips reward catalog

Boss Loot includes 50 individual items, including four armor pieces per metal tier from bronze through dragon. Full armor sets are not awarded as a single drop.

Clue Casket includes 742 distinct Grand Exchange-mapped item rewards drawn from the [OSRS Wiki Treasure Trails casket tables](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Reward_casket_(beginner)) across all six tiers. Duplicate items across tiers are merged. Coins, untradeable unlocks, and clue progression items are excluded. Challenger Rips odds are custom.

Pet Hunt includes 83 base pet entries from the [OSRS Wiki pet tables](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pet) and two legacy material drops. Recolors, metamorphoses, and dog breeds or life stages are grouped under their base pet. Custom GP values for boss, skilling, and activity pets use the Wiki's September 2026 ownership snapshot where available. Pets without a count use a middle tier and quest or event companions use a separate low-value tier.

Tradeable guide prices use item IDs and the [OSRS Wiki real-time Grand Exchange feed](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:REALTIME). The guide averages the available latest high and low trades and timestamps the older included trade. The database includes a deployment snapshot, and newer quotes are cached in the browser. Quotes older than 24 hours are marked stale. Player market listing prices are independently set by sellers.

Legacy daily claims only recorded a UTC date. The migration uses midnight UTC on that date as the starting time for the final legacy cooldown. New claims record the actual server timestamp.
